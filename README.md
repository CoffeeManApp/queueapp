# CoffeeMan Queue - Restaurant Queue mini app

A simple and minimalist web app that helps restaurants manage walk-in queues. Customers enter their details on a phone or iPad at the front, get a queue number, and wait to be called. Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## 🚀 Features

✅ Customers check in with name, WhatsApp number, and party size.  
✅ Automatic queue numbers (A001, A002, ...) with the number of groups ahead.  
✅ Staff view to call, seat, or skip customers in one tap.  
✅ "Now calling" board so everyone can see the current number.  
✅ One-tap WhatsApp message to customers who are not around.  
✅ Stored locally in your browser (no account needed).  

## 📱 How to Use

**Customer (Check in tab)**

Enter your name and WhatsApp number.

Select your party size.

Tap "Get my number" and wait for your number to be called.

**Staff (Staff tab)**

Tap "Call next & WhatsApp" to call the next customer. WhatsApp opens with the message ready, then tap send.

If a customer is not around, tap "WhatsApp" on their row to send the message again.

Tap "Seat" when the customer arrives, or "No show" if they do not come.

Tap "Reset for new day" at the bottom to clear the queue and restart numbering.

## 🔐 Data Storage & Privacy

All data is saved locally on your device using Local Storage.
This means customer data stays private and is not uploaded anywhere.
If you clear browser cache or use another device, the queue will not carry over. Use one iPad or phone for both Check in and Staff.

# CoffeeMan排队系统 餐厅排队迷你小程序

一个简单极简的网页应用，帮助餐厅管理现场排队。顾客在门口的手机或 iPad 上填写资料，领取号码，然后等待叫号。基于 HTML、CSS 和 JavaScript 开发。

## 🚀 功能特点

✅ 顾客输入姓名、WhatsApp 号码和用餐人数即可排队。  
✅ 自动生成排队号码（A001、A002……），并显示前面还有几组。  
✅ 员工页面一键叫号、入座或跳过。  
✅ “正在叫号”显示板，大家都能看到当前号码。  
✅ 顾客不在现场时，一键发送 WhatsApp 通知。  
✅ 数据保存在你的浏览器本地（无需注册账号）。  

## 📱 使用方法

**顾客（Check in 页面）**

输入姓名和 WhatsApp 号码。

选择用餐人数。

点击“Get my number”领取号码，等待叫号。

**员工（Staff 页面）**

点击“Call next & WhatsApp”叫下一位。WhatsApp 会打开并带好消息，点击发送即可。

如果顾客不在，点击该行的“WhatsApp”再次发送通知。

顾客到达后点击“Seat”，没来则点击“No show”。

点击底部“Reset for new day”清空队列并重新编号。

## 🔐 数据存储与隐私

所有数据都保存在你设备的本地存储（Local Storage）。
顾客资料不会上传到任何地方，完全私密。
如果你清空浏览器缓存或换设备，队列不会同步。请用同一台 iPad 或手机进行排队登记和员工操作。
